# Contacting the IT helpdesk

## How to reach us
The fastest way is the Service Portal. You can also email helpdesk@northwind.example, call extension 4357, or message IT Helpdesk in Teams. The helpdesk is staffed Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm IST. A 24/7 on-call engineer handles major outages. Walk-in support is at the IT desk on Level 2 from 9am to 6pm.

## Ticket priorities and response times
P1 is an outage affecting many people, with a first response within 30 minutes. P2 means one person is blocked from working, with a first response within 4 business hours. P3 is a question or a routine request, with a first response within 2 business days. Mention how many people are affected so the ticket is prioritised correctly.
