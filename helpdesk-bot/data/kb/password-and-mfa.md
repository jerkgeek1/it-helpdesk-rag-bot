# Passwords and multi-factor authentication

## Resetting your password
Go to id.northwind.example/reset, enter your work email and approve the prompt in the Authenticator app. Passwords expire every 180 days and must be at least 14 characters long. You cannot reuse any of your last 10 passwords. After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account locks for 15 minutes.

## Locked out and no MFA device
If you cannot approve the Authenticator prompt, call the helpdesk on extension 4357 or open a ticket from another device. Your manager must confirm your identity. Once confirmed, IT issues a Temporary Access Pass that is valid for 8 hours, which lets you sign in and register a new MFA device.

## Setting up MFA on a new phone
Do this before you wipe or replace your old phone. Sign in at id.northwind.example/security, choose Add new device, and scan the QR code with the Authenticator app on the new phone. Once the new phone works, remove the old device from the same page. If your old phone is already lost, use the Locked out and no MFA device steps instead. Hardware security keys are available from the IT desk for people who cannot use a phone.
