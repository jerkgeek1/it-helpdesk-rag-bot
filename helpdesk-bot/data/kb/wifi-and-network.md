# Wi-Fi and office network

## Wi-Fi networks in the office
Northwind-Corp is the network for employees. Company laptops join it automatically because a certificate is installed at enrolment. Northwind-Guest is for visitors and personal phones. The guest password changes every day and is available from reception, and each guest session lasts 4 hours. Northwind-IoT is reserved for company devices such as room displays and must not be used for personal devices.

## Laptop will not connect to Wi-Fi
Forget the network and join it again. Open Company Portal and choose Sync to refresh your certificate. If the error says the certificate has expired, syncing Company Portal normally fixes it within a few minutes. If it still fails, open a ticket and include a screenshot of the error.

## Wired network and docks
Every desk in the Bengaluru and Pune offices has an Ethernet dock. Plug in the dock cable and the laptop connects to the wired network automatically. Wired connections are faster and more stable for video calls than Wi-Fi.
