# Security and phishing

## Reporting a suspicious email
Use the Report Phishing button in Outlook, or forward the email as an attachment to phishing@northwind.example. Do not click links or open attachments in the message.

## If you clicked a link or entered your password
Change your password immediately. Then report it to security@northwind.example and the helpdesk within 15 minutes, even if you are not sure it was malicious. Reporting quickly limits the damage and you will not be blamed.

## What IT will never ask
IT staff never ask for your password or your MFA codes, by phone, chat or email. Never share an MFA code with anyone.

## USB drives and data classification
USB storage is blocked by default. If you need an exception, request one in the Service Portal with your manager's approval. Data is labelled Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted data must never be emailed outside the company.

## Password manager
Northwind provides the VaultKey password manager. Install it from Software Center and use it to store work passwords instead of saving them in the browser.
