# Hardware, loaners and onboarding

## New hire equipment
Your laptop is ready on your first day. Engineering and design roles get a MacBook Pro 14 inch and other roles get a Lenovo ThinkPad T14, unless your manager requests a different model. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years.

## Monitors, headsets and home office
Every employee can request one 27 inch monitor. Headsets are available on request from the IT desk. Remote employees receive a one-time home-office stipend of 15,000 INR for a desk setup.

## Loaner laptops
If your laptop is being repaired, you can borrow a loaner laptop from the IT desk on Level 2 between 9am and 6pm. Loaners can be kept for up to 5 working days.

## Returning equipment when you leave
Return all equipment within 5 working days of your last day. IT emails you a prepaid courier label. Equipment that is not returned is reported to your manager and to finance.
