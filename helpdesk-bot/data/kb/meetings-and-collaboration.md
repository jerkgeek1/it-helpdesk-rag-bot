# Meetings and collaboration

## Which tool to use
Microsoft Teams is the standard tool for meetings and chat. Zoom is only for external calls when a customer requires it, and you need a Zoom licence, which you can request in the Service Portal. Teams meetings support up to 300 participants. For larger audiences, request a Teams Town Hall from IT.

## Camera or microphone not working in Teams
Open Teams Settings, then Devices, and select the correct camera and microphone. Check that the operating system allows Teams to use them. On a Mac, also allow Teams under Privacy and Security. Restart Teams. If it still fails, clear the Teams cache and sign in again.

## Screen sharing on Mac
Screen sharing on a Mac needs the Screen Recording permission. Open System Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Screen Recording, enable Teams and restart the app.

## Recordings
Teams recordings are saved to OneDrive or SharePoint and are deleted automatically after 60 days. Download a recording before then if you need to keep it.

## Meeting rooms
Book rooms with the Room Finder in Outlook. Each room has a display with HDMI and USB-C cables. If a room display does not work, call the helpdesk and mention the room name.
