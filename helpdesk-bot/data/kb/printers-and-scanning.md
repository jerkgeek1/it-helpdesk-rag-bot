# Printers and scanning

## Printing with Secure Print
All printing uses the NW-Secure-Print queue. Send your document to that queue, walk to any printer and tap your badge to release the job. Jobs that are not released are deleted after 24 hours. To add the printer to your laptop, open Company Portal and choose Printers.

## Colour printing and supplies
Colour printers are in Print Room 2 on Level 3. Other printers are black and white only. To report an empty toner cartridge or missing paper, open a ticket in the Service Portal under Facilities, then Printer supplies.

## Paper jams
Do not pull jammed paper by force, because it can damage the printer. Open the marked panels and remove visible paper gently. If the jam does not clear, call the helpdesk and mention the printer name shown on its label.

## Scanning
Use scan to email at any printer. Scans are sent only to your own work email address and can contain up to 50 pages per scan.
