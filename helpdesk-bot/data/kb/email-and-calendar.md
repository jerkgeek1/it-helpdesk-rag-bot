# Email and calendar (Outlook)

## Mailbox size and attachments
Every mailbox has 50 GB of storage. The maximum attachment size is 25 MB. For larger files, upload the file to OneDrive and share a link instead of attaching it.

## Shared mailboxes and out-of-office
To get access to a shared mailbox, submit an access request in the Service Portal. The mailbox appears in Outlook within 60 minutes after approval. To set an out-of-office message in Outlook, go to File, then Automatic Replies, and choose the dates and message.

## Recovering deleted emails
Deleted emails stay in the Deleted Items folder for 30 days. After that, choose Recover Deleted Items From Server in Outlook to restore messages for up to 14 more days. After those 14 days, messages cannot be recovered.

## Email on your phone
Only the Outlook mobile app is supported on phones. The built-in Mail apps on iOS and Android are not supported and may be blocked. Sign in to Outlook mobile with your work account and approve the MFA prompt.
