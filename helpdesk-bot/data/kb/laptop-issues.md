# Laptop problems

## Laptop is slow
Restart first, since pending updates are the most common cause of slowness. Restart at least once a week. Then check that your disk has less than 85 percent used, close extra browser tabs, and run Health Check in Company Portal. If it is still slow after that, open a ticket.

## Updates and restarts
Security updates install in the background. After an update is downloaded you have 3 days before a restart is forced. Save your work before the deadline so you do not lose it.

## Battery problems
Run the battery report from Company Portal. Northwind replaces a battery when its maximum capacity falls below 60 percent of the original capacity. Open a hardware ticket and attach the report.

## Blue screen or laptop will not start
Hold the power button for 15 seconds to force a shutdown, then start it again. If it still does not boot, or the blue screen keeps coming back, call the helpdesk. Do not try to reinstall the operating system yourself.

## Lost or stolen laptop
Report a lost or stolen laptop within 1 hour to the helpdesk and to security@northwind.example. IT will lock the device and can wipe it remotely. All laptops use full-disk encryption, and a BitLocker or FileVault recovery key can be retrieved from the Service Portal if you are asked for one.
